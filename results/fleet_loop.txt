CONTAINERIZED FLEET -- full self-improvement loop under a workload shift
========================================================================

2 serving replicas + router + async trainer + canary controller, all in Docker.
CPU inference (this host has no nvidia-container-toolkit), Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct fp32.
300 requests, workload shifted code+chat -> medical+legal at the halfway point.
Captured 2026-11-04T23:26:41Z

WHAT TO LOOK FOR: the fleet cold-starts with NO speculation, trains candidates
from its own live traffic, and the SLO gate refuses every one of them on a
statistically significant p95 regression. That is the correct outcome here:
a CPU target forward is compute-bound, so a wider speculative batch costs real
time, and the trainer's objective (tokens per target forward) does not price
that in. The gate caught the mismatch and production never regressed.
On GPU, where the forward is memory-bound, the same drafters do speed things up
-- see results/benchmark.txt.
